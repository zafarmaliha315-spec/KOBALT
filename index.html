The Story

Built in
the static.

KOBALT started recording in a converted laundry room in 2019, trading verses over borrowed beats before the money existed for anything else. The sound sits between boom-bap grit and modern low-end — drums that hit like they mean it, hooks that stick after one pass.

Three self-released EPs and one independent tape later, the catalog runs on word of mouth: no label, no shortcuts, just a growing list of cities that know the words already.
